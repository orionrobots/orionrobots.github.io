This one's a bit different from my usual posts - it's not my own book, but it's close enough to what I care about that I wanted to share it anyway.

My publisher, Packt, has put together the Embedded Firmware Developer Toolkit on Humble Bundle: a collection of 15+ books covering microcontrollers, C/C++, ARM Cortex-M, RTOS, embedded Linux, device drivers, debugging, RISC-V, and more.

If you've read any of my robotics books, you'll know I mostly work a layer up from this - Python, Raspberry Pi, sensors, computer vision. But every robot ultimately depends on firmware running well below that layer, and understanding it makes you a better robotics builder, not just a better programmer.

There's a good range in this bundle, from foundational material through to deeper system-level topics, so whether you're just starting to look under the hood or already comfortable there and want to go deeper, there's likely something useful in it.
